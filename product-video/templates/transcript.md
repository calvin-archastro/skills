# Narration: <Journey title>

Scenario: [scenario.md](scenario.md) · Screen take: [script.md](script.md) ·
Lines: L1–L<n> · Planned: <sum> s

Every word the finished video says. Read it after the screen take, once the
`[after take]` lines are filled in from what really happened.

## How to read it

1. Read it from the Splice teleprompter, looking at the camera.
2. After a flub, pause and read the whole line again. The last reading wins.
3. Read at a relaxed pace. The picture is cut to fit your voice, not the
   other way round.
4. Without the teleprompter: say the line's number ("L3"), pause, read the
   line, pause about two seconds; after a flub, say the number again.
