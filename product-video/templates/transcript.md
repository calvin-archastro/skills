## How to read it

1. Look at the camera. Keep this page just under or beside the lens.
2. Say the line's number ("L3"), pause, read the line, pause about two
   seconds.
3. After a flub, say the number again and read the whole line again. The
   last reading wins.
4. Read at a relaxed pace. The picture is cut to fit your voice, not the
   other way round.
